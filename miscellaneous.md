# Miscellaneous

## Existence of Glitched Pets

Glitched pets are hybrids of Rainbow and Dark Matter pets. By theory, these pets came from the Dark Matter Egg. With a chance of hatching a Rainbow pet of 0.5% / 2% with the Gamepass, it was possible to obtain these.

## How Merging Pets Work

The exact merge order is unknown. The game appears to select an amount of the same pet type, removes them, and generates a pet of a higher upgrade type with a level ranging from its minimum and maximum.

- Regular to Gold: 3x power
- Gold to Rainbow: 2x power
- Rainbow to Dark Matter: 3x power

## Coin Damage and XP Rewards

A pet is estimated to attack every approximately 0.13 seconds.
